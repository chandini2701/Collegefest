About Our Celebration

Sri Vasavi Institute of Engineering and Technology proudly celebrates its Annual Anniversary with students, faculty, alumni and distinguished guests.

The celebration includes cultural programs, technical competitions, music, dance, games, photography, alumni interaction, prize distribution, food stalls and many exciting activities.
